## Author

The framework is developed by [Steve Isaacs](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sjisaacs), drawing on experience in global health market shaping. It is written in a personal capacity. The views here are the author's own and do not represent any employer or partner organization.

## Status

This is version 2.0, a draft published in November 2026. Version 2.0 reorganizes the framework around cost-effectiveness, corrects examples from earlier versions, and replaces the original single-page site with one that can be read, linked and indexed page by page.

## How to cite

Isaacs, S. (2026). *The Impact Financing Framework*, version 2.0. https://impactfinancingframework.org

## Feedback

Corrections, cases and challenges are welcome. Please open an issue on the [project repository](https://github.com/sjisaacs/impact-financing-framework/issues). Errors that are corrected are recorded in the changelog below.

## Changelog

- **2.0 draft, November 2026.** Reoriented around durable impact per concessional dollar. Added the wedge geometry, the payer layer, durability by lever, a worked cost-effectiveness example with a calculator, inline definitions of key terms, and a rebuilt six-step diagnostic with a back-of-envelope ratio. Focused the instrument catalogue. Kept the landscape illustrations, the original terminology and the visual design. Corrected examples, including the pneumococcal advance market commitment, the Educate Girls impact bond and African Risk Capacity.
- **1.0, May 2026.** First public version.
